Our Story

A little cake house, a whole lot of heart

Nancy Cake House is a patisserie and cake shop in Madhavaram, Chennai, built on one simple idea: every celebration deserves a cake that's as beautiful as it is delicious.

Every order is made from scratch, by hand and in small batches with good ingredients. Your cake is designed around your theme, your flavour and your moment, and baked fresh for your day.
